## QAISER

New candidate | Computer Science; Engineering

Study type: Systematic literature reviews in software engineering

Assesses the methodological quality of completed reviews. It adapts AMSTAR 2 to software engineering and contains 15 items.

Evidence: Four external experts reviewed the instrument; the authors demonstrated it on six reviews. This supports content and usability, but does not establish broad independent reliability.

For review: Prioritize for the software engineering gap. Check whether assessment of secondary studies fits the repository scope.

- https://doi.org/10.37190/e-Inf230105

- https://www.e-informatyka.pl/einformatica/volumes/volume-2023/issue-1/article-5/

## ROBFEAD

New candidate | Engineering; computational biomechanics

Study type: Finite element analysis studies in dentistry

Six domains and 22 guiding questions assess risk of bias in dental finite element research. This directly addresses simulation research in a narrow engineering setting.

Evidence: The development paper describes a validation phase. Its abstract does not establish a general interrater or intrarater coefficient; the full validation methods should be checked.

For review: Prioritize for engineering. Any use in general finite element physics would be an adaptation requiring evaluation.

- https://doi.org/10.1080/10255842.2022.2148465

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36475384/

## ToxRTool

New candidate | Chemistry; toxicology

Study type: In vivo and in vitro toxicological studies

Evaluates the inherent quality of toxicological data using explicit criteria and reliability categories. In this context, data reliability means study quality, not interrater reliability of the instrument.

Evidence: Development paper and a 2015 external evaluation are available. Read both before making a general validation claim.

For review: Review the study-quality criteria. Do not treat its category score as interchangeable with domain-specific risk of bias.

- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.toxlet.2009.05.013

- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yrtph.2015.03.005

- https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/what-we-do/scientific-tools-and-databases/toxrtool-toxicological-data-reliability-assessment-tool_en

## SciRAP for in vivo toxicity studies

New candidate | Chemistry; toxicology

Study type: Animal toxicity experiments

Evaluates methodological and reporting quality separately from relevance. The quality component is potentially in scope; the relevance component needs a separate decision.

Evidence: Expert testing and refinement reported in 2018. A later systematic-review comparison explicitly states that it is not a validation of SciRAP in that context.

For review: Include only if Leila accepts the methodological quality component. Keep relevance judgments separate.

- https://doi.org/10.1002/jat.3648

- https://ki.se/en/imm/scirap-science-in-risk-assessment-and-policy/scirap-tools/the-scirap-tool-for-in-vivo-toxicity-studies

## CRED including the sediment and soil extension

New candidate | Agriculture; Chemistry; ecotoxicology

Study type: Aquatic, sediment and soil ecotoxicity studies

The evaluation method has explicit study-quality criteria and separate relevance criteria. It also has reporting recommendations, which should not be mistaken for the assessment tool.

Evidence: A ring test compared CRED with Klimisch. A 2024 extension adds soil and sediment considerations. Transfer of the original evaluation results to the extension is not established here.

For review: Potential fit for pesticide and soil ecotoxicity evidence. This is not a universal agricultural field-trial tool.

- https://doi.org/10.1002/etc.3259

- https://doi.org/10.1186/s12302-016-0073-x

## RoBiNT

New candidate | Psychology; rehabilitation

Study type: Single-case experimental designs and n-of-1 trials

Contains separate internal-validity and external-validity/interpretation subscales. The internal-validity component directly addresses methodological bias.

Evidence: 2013 evaluation supports discriminative validity and excellent interrater reliability. Reported total-score ICC was 0.90 for experienced raters and 0.88 for trained novices in the evaluated sample.

For review: Prioritize for single-case psychology research. Decide whether to record the full instrument or distinguish its subscales.

- https://doi.org/10.1080/09602011.2013.824383

## Cambridge Quality Checklists

New candidate | Law; criminology; Psychology

Study type: Studies of correlates, risk factors and causal risk factors for offending

Assesses methodological quality of empirical criminological studies. It is a study-appraisal instrument, not a tool for judging legal arguments or case law.

Evidence: 2012 study evaluated 60 studies of disrupted families and offending, reporting high interrater reliability and generally larger effects in lower-quality studies. Authors recommend refinement.

For review: Suitable candidate for the empirical law and criminal-justice gap. Do not generalize to doctrinal legal scholarship.

- https://doi.org/10.1002/cbm.1837

- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3660786/

## QUADAS-3

Version update candidate | Diagnostic research; selected medical physics applications

Study type: Diagnostic test accuracy studies

Updated diagnostic accuracy risk-of-bias instrument. Adds an ideal test accuracy trial concept, estimate-level assessment and an analysis domain.

Evidence: Development publication exists. This review has not established independent psychometric validation of the new version. QUADAS-2 estimates must not be transferred automatically.

For review: Consider an update alongside the existing QUADAS-2 entry. Medical physics use is an inferred design-based fit, not evidence of validation across physics.

- https://doi.org/10.7326/ANNALS-25-02104

- https://www.bristol.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/projects/quadas/

## PROBAST+AI

Version update candidate | Computer Science; prediction research

Study type: Clinical prediction models using regression or artificial intelligence

Updates quality, risk-of-bias and applicability assessment for prediction model development and evaluation. It does not validate arbitrary machine learning benchmarks.

Evidence: 2025 development paper is available. Independent reliability evidence for this exact revision was not established in this review.

For review: Prioritize for the clinical AI subset. Preserve the distinction between general computer science and health prediction models.

- https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-082505

- https://www.probast.org/probast_ai

## JBI textual evidence checklists

Three version update candidates | Law and policy; qualitative evidence

Study type: Narrative evidence, expert opinion and policy text

JBI now provides three separate checklists in place of the older general text/opinion approach. They assess authenticity, credibility and argument structure.

Evidence: 2025 methodology paper explains development and appraisal. An independent reliability evaluation was not located.

For review: Review each checklist for scope before adding it. Policy-text credibility is not necessarily methodological quality or risk of bias in an empirical study. Compare with text/opinion and NOTARI rows.

- https://doi.org/10.11124/JBIES-24-00293

- https://jbi.global/critical-appraisal-tools

- https://jbi.global/news/critically-appraising-diverse-textual-sources

Decision: Add / Update / Hold / Exclude

## CEE Critical Appraisal Tool formal version

Version update candidate | Agriculture; environmental sciences

Study type: Comparative quantitative environmental studies of causal effects

Directly evaluates risk of bias using criteria that feed an overall judgment. Its environmental scope can cover relevant agronomy questions after design-specific assessment.

Evidence: Formal version published 11 October 2026 after prototype use, user feedback and revision. The paper calls for further evaluation; a general reliability claim is premature.

For review: Update the existing version 0.3 record after comparing items and confirming the version. This is not a wholly new tool family.

- https://doi.org/10.1186/s13750-026-00394-9

- https://environmentalevidence.org/cee-critical-appraisal-tool/

## SciRAPepi

New candidate | Chemistry; environmental epidemiology

Study type: Cross-sectional, case-control, nested case-control and cohort studies of chemical exposure

Evaluates study reliability and relevance for chemical hazard and risk assessment. Keep methodological evaluation separate from relevance to a policy question.

Evidence: 2026 development paper reports expert testing by 37 epidemiologists and risk assessors on four studies, followed by refinement. Wider independent validation remains open.

For review: Consider as a separate study-design instrument within the SciRAP family. Confirm which components meet the repository scope.

- https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.5c11558

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41543517/
