How to read this review

Each existing entry was searched using its name and validity, validated, reliability, inter-rater reliability and intra-rater reliability. Findings draw on relevant primary publications and developer guidance; some rely on abstracts when full text was unavailable. This is a targeted web review, not an exhaustive database search.

Development and expert consensus support different claims from empirical validity or reliability testing. “Not located” means the evidence was not established in this review. Findings for an older version do not automatically validate a revision.

The workbook records 126 distinct cell-and-URL combinations, including embedded hyperlinks. Access restrictions, disabled pages and confirmed HTTP errors are distinguished. Some replacements provide a stable article or DOI record while full tool access remains unverified.

General physics remains a search gap. The limited medical physics and computational biomechanics mappings are design-based possibilities, not validation across physics. New candidates remain separate from the existing collection pending Leila’s review.
