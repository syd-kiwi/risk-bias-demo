VIRGINIA TECH

Leila Ledbetter

Evidence Synthesis Information Scientist
University Libraries

Leila is an Evidence Synthesis Information Scientist at Virginia Tech and a co-author of this repository. She holds a Master of Library and Information Science from the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee and a bachelor’s degree in biology from Marietta College.

DUKE UNIVERSITY

Stephanie “Steph” Hendren

Research and Education Librarian
Medical Center Library & Archives

Steph is a Research and Education Librarian at Duke University and a co-author of this repository. She supports Duke’s physical therapy and occupational therapy programs, teaches classes, conducts systematic reviews, and provides research and project consultations. She holds an MLIS in Library and Information Studies and a bachelor’s degree in English literature from the University of Wisconsin–Madison.
